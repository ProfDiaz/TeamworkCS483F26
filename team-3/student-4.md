## strategy: practice coding daily
one of the best ways to study computer science is to practice coding every day instead of doing everything at the last minute before the exam.

## explanation
practicing a little every day helps us understand the concepts better and also makes it easier to remember things. it also helps us find our mistakes and learn from them.

## example
for example, if i am learning python, i can spend around 30 minutes every day practicing different coding problems and going over the mistakes i made. this will help me improve my coding skills and prepare better for exams.
