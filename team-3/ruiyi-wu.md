# Active Recall and Spaced Review

**Contributor:** Ruiyi Wu (`RayWu1037`)

## Strategy

Turn each study topic into a question. Close your notes and try to explain or solve it from memory before checking the answer. Review the questions again after a day, a few days later, and about a week later. Spend extra practice on the questions you missed.

## Why it helps

Active recall shows which ideas you can retrieve without looking at notes. Spacing reviews over time gives you repeated chances to retrieve the material instead of concentrating all your review in one session.

## Example: Recursion

After studying recursion, close your notes and explain the base case and recursive case. For factorial:

- Base case: `factorial(0) = 1`
- Recursive case: `factorial(n) = n * factorial(n - 1)` for `n > 0`

Try writing the function from memory, then check it with inputs such as `0` and `4`. If you forget why the base case is needed, turn that point into a question and revisit it during your next spaced review.
