## Tip: Study in short focused blocks (Pomodoro Technique)

Explanation: Instead of studying for hours straight, work for 25 minutes with no distractions, then take a 5-minute break. After four blocks, take a longer 15–30 minute break. 

## Example

When preparing for a exam, set a 25-minute timer and work only on practice problems from one topic, like stacks. When the timer ends, take a 5-minute break, then start the next block on a different topic.
